# My First Money Adventure — *Little Money Explorers, Book 1*

A complete, KDP-ready 32-page picture book manuscript for children ages 3–5, teaching that **everything around us exists because someone helped create it, and money is simply a thank-you for helping people.**

  - Back matter activities: Helping Chart, Thank-You card, Draw Your Helper, coloring pages, maze, matching game
  - Back cover copy

  - Illustration Consistency Guide (character bible, palette, lighting, composition rules) for the illustrator or an AI image tool
  - Book 2 series setup / cliffhanger notes

## Story premise

Milu finds a mysterious glowing coin that can't buy toys — instead it takes her and her companion Pico on an adventure where touching everyday objects (bread, milk, a bus, a book, a shirt, a hospital, a school, fruit) reveals the people who worked together to make them possible: a farmer, a driver, a teacher, a tailor, a doctor, a builder, a shopkeeper.
